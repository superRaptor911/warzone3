WORLD ART PREVIEW

Everything here is drawn by client/js/gfx/tileset.ts against the shipped Kenney tilesheet — the same code the tile atlas bakes from, so what this page shows is what a match shows. Geometry and materials come from shared/maps.ts via buildMap, so this is also the fastest way to judge a level edit without playing it. Wall composites are drawn filling their cell on purpose: the painted edge is the collision edge, and the dashed boxes in the props row mark the tile a prop makes solid.
